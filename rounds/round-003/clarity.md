# Clarity & Exposition — Round 3

**Recommendation:** minor polish
The through-line, signposting, and cross-register discipline all hold; what remains is sentence- and term-level tightening, not restructuring.

## Strengths
- `01-context.prose.md` — "What This Tutorial Covers" states the WHAT→WHY→HOW→WHAT ELSE roadmap explicitly and maps it onto the section arc before the reader hits any architecture, and the promised capability list matches `topic.md` and `04-conclusion.prose.md` word for word. This is the kind of signposting the brief asks for and it is genuinely there.
- `03-content.prose.md` consistently orients the reader back to the anchor figure at every stage of the walkthrough ("the stack shown in Figure 2," "the first block inside each layer of Figure 2," "pictured whole in Figure 4") — a through-line that survives seven `##` units without losing the reader's place.
- `03-content`'s self-attention passage closes the loop the framework asks reviewers to watch for: it explicitly names self-attention as "the mechanism behind a fixed context window," cashing in the context-window claim `02-motivation` made a section earlier instead of leaving it as an assertion.
- `slidecontent.md` across all four sections reads as talking points written for the medium, not compressed prose — short claims, one figure, one idea per unit — which is the discipline the project's split-register decision was meant to produce.

## Weaknesses
- `02-motivation.prose.md` — "Every capability and every failure mode your agent exhibits traces back to this mechanism." "This mechanism" sits between two different referents: the sentence before it describes the LLM-plus-actions orchestration loop, but the rest of the paragraph (hallucination, context window, frozen weights) is actually about next-token prediction, introduced only in the sentence that follows. A first-time reader parses "this mechanism" as the orchestration loop just defined, not the architecture fact that's about to explain hallucination.
- `04-conclusion.prose.md` — "**agentic tool use**, where the model's output drives actions in the world, not just more text" is listed among "fronts" of where the field is still headed. But `02-motivation.prose.md` opens by defining "an agentic system" as already, presently, "an LLM plus the ability to take actions" — the course's foundational premise, not an emerging development. The same term does different jobs in the two sections: established fact in §2, open frontier in §4.
- `03-content.slidecontent.md` § "From the Stack to Next-Token Probabilities" names the operation as "**Linear + Softmax**," but the parallel passage in `03-content.prose.md` § "Stacking Layers to a Next-Token Distribution" only says "one more projection... turning it into a probability distribution" — the document never uses or defines "softmax" or "linear" anywhere. A reader who hits the slide term with no document to check it against has to take it on faith.
- `03-content.prose.md` — the jump from § "From Encoder-Decoder to Decoder-Only" (ends on why decoder-only scaled) to § "Tokens and Embeddings" (opens mid-mechanism, "every token id is looked up...") has no sentence marking the shift from architecture description to layer-by-layer walkthrough. Every other section transition in this document is signposted; this is the one seam that isn't.

## Actionable
1. `02-motivation.prose.md` § "Why Architecture Understanding Matters for Agents" — replace "traces back to this mechanism" with a phrase that names what it refers to, e.g. "traces back to the next-token-prediction mechanism underneath it" (*why:* removes a pronoun that currently points at the wrong sentence and forces a re-read of the paragraph's own topic sentence).
2. `04-conclusion.prose.md` § "Where the Field Is Headed" — either drop "agentic tool use" from the forward-looking list (it's already the section's established premise, not a frontier) or reword it to something forward-looking it hasn't already claimed, e.g. framing *more autonomous* multi-step tool use rather than tool use itself (*why:* stops the same term from meaning "settled foundation" in §2 and "open frontier" in §4).
3. `03-content.slidecontent.md` § "From the Stack to Next-Token Probabilities" — either add "softmax" to `03-content.prose.md`'s parallel sentence (one clause: "a **softmax** turns those scores into a probability distribution") or drop the named operation from the slide and keep it at the same level of abstraction as the document (*why:* a viewer flipping between slide and document currently meets a term on one that the other never backs up).
4. `03-content.prose.md` — add one clause at the end of "From Encoder-Decoder to Decoder-Only" or the start of "Tokens and Embeddings" marking the pivot from architecture description to the walkthrough proper, e.g. "From here, follow one token through the stack, stage by stage." (*why:* this is the only section boundary in the document without a signpost, and it's exactly the boundary where the reader shifts from being told about the architecture to being walked through it).
