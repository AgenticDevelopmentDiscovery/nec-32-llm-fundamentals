# Clarity & Exposition — Round 2

**Recommendation:** minor polish
prose reads clean and the WHAT→WHY→HOW→WHAT ELSE arc closes the loop it opens, but two real in-document defects and unrepaired spine/proposal drift need attention before the next round.

## Strengths

- The document delivers on its own roadmap almost verbatim: `01-context`'s closing promise ("you'll be able to trace a token through the architecture and explain why an agent behaves the way it does, not just how to prompt one") and `topic.md`'s "What the reader will be able to do" both land, nearly word for word, in `04-conclusion`'s "What You Can Do Now." A reader who reads only the first and last sections would recognize the contract was kept.
- One worked example — "The cat sat on the mat because it was tired" — is reused across `Self-Attention`, its figure caption, both demo prose slides, and the `demo-attention-map` caption. That's a genuine pedagogical thread: the reader watches the same sentence get harder to explain and then watches it explained on a real model, instead of meeting a new example each time.
- Jargon discipline is consistent everywhere except the one lapse noted below: **Transformer**, **attention**, **context window**, **embedding table**, *query*/*key*/*value*, **heads**, **residual connection**, **normalization** are all bolded and defined in the same sentence they're introduced, not assumed.

## Weaknesses

- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — "cross-attention" appears exactly once in the whole document, inside the caption of "The Decoder-Only Stack, Visualized": *"Drop the encoder and cross-attention, keep everything else..."* The mechanism itself is described two slides earlier ("attending both to its own previous outputs and to the encoder's representation") but never named. A reader meets the term "cross-attention" for the first time in a figure caption, with nothing before it to anchor the word to the mechanism it names.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — figure attribution is inconsistent across the three diagrams. "The Encoder-Decoder Stack, Visualized" is captioned "Adapted from Vaswani et al. (2017), Figure 1." — but "The Decoder-Only Stack, Visualized" (derived from that same source figure) and "Self-Attention, Visualized" carry no citation at all. `topic.md`'s Scope states plainly that "each figure carries a citation back to its source paper"; two of three don't. This isn't a nitpick about formatting — per the note accompanying this round's materials, the figures shipped are now hand-drawn originals rather than the reused papers' figures `topic.md` describes, and that pivot (and its citation implications) isn't recorded anywhere a reader or reviewer can check it.
- `sections/03-content.concepts.md` — the Decisions log is now factually wrong about the shipped document: it says "Figures from 'Attention Is All You Need' and the GPT paper line are reused as-is, not redrawn" and "8 `##` units confirmed as the right granularity," and describes the demo as "Not yet built; the prose slide is a description of the plan, not the demo itself." The prose has hand-drawn originals, 11 headings, and a demo with real captured output. None of this is a defect in the prose — it's a defect in the spine, which is supposed to be the record of what was decided and why, and right now tells the next reader something false about the document it sits beside.
- `sections/02-motivation.concepts.md` — same failure mode, smaller scale: the Decisions log still discusses a 3-heading structure ("What Goes Wrong Without It" as a single unit), while the shipped prose has 5 headings (Why Architecture Understanding Matters, Hallucination, Context Window, Frozen Weights, Sampling). There's no Decision entry explaining the split, so a reader of the spine can't tell whether the extra headings were a deliberate slide-overflow fix or drift.

(*why:* a caption is the worst place to meet new vocabulary — there's no room around it to explain.)
2. `sections/03-content.prose.md` + `topic.md` § Scope — either add source citations to the decoder-only and self-attention captions to match the encoder-decoder one, or update `topic.md`'s Scope/Decisions to state the actual policy now in effect (original diagrams, attributed conceptually but not "adapted from Figure N"), and make the captions consistent with whichever policy is chosen. (*why:* right now the document silently breaks a promise it made about itself, and a careful reader will notice one figure is cited and two aren't with no visible reason.)
3. `sections/03-content.concepts.md` § Decisions — rewrite the stale entries (figure sourcing, 8-unit granularity, demo not-yet-built) to describe what actually shipped, and add one line explaining why the section grew from 8 to 11 units (overflow found once figures/demo landed, per the round note) instead of leaving that reasoning only in an out-of-band note.
